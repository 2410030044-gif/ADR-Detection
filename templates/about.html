About This Project

Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) are unintended effects of medications that can harm patients, increase healthcare costs, and sometimes be life‑threatening.
Research Goal

This project explores how Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning can automatically detect ADRs from patient text and medical records.

Methods

The live ADR classifier uses Sentence-BERT embeddings from all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and a scikit-learn Logistic Regression model.
