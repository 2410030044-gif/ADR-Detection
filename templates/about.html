About This Project

Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) are unintended effects of medications that can harm patients, increase healthcare costs, and sometimes be life‑threatening.
Research Goal

This project explores how Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning can automatically detect ADRs from patient text and medical records.

Methods

We compared Bag‑of‑Words and Naive Bayes with advanced transformer‑based models such as Sentence‑BERT.

Model Performance

Baseline ROC-AUC: 0.84
Sentence-BERT ROC-AUC: 0.86
Precision: 90%
F1 Score: 88%
Impact

By automating ADR detection, healthcare systems can identify risks earlier, improve patient safety, and reduce costs.
